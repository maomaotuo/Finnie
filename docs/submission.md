# Finnie submission checklist

**Project title**

Finnie — Multi-Agent AI Finance Assistant

**Project description**

Finnie helps learners understand financial concepts, analyze portfolios, explore market data, and plan savings goals. LangGraph routes questions to six specialists. Finance Q&A and Tax Education share a FAISS knowledge base, Market and News combine quotes with cited reporting, and Portfolio and Goals use Python calculators. The Streamlit app supports typed and voice questions. The local MCP server exposes portfolio, savings, and quote tools.
